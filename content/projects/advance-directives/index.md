Investigates the statutory conflicts and institutional reforms concerning living wills, healthcare proxy decision-making, intentional guardianship, and end-of-life self-determination across China, South Korea, the U.K., the Isle of Man, and Canada.

<!--more-->

**Key outputs (1 SSCI + 6 KCI articles)**

- SSCI (JCR Q1, IF = 3.8): *BMC Medical Ethics* (2026), first and corresponding author.
- KCI (2025, first author): *Journal of China Studies* (healthcare proxy decision-making); *Asia Pacific Journal of Health Law & Ethics* (two articles: comparative end-of-life self-determination boundaries; living wills for pregnant patients); *Kangwon Law Review* (intentional guardianship and advance directives); *Journal of International Business Transactions Law* (patient self-determination rights); *Chinese Law Review* (legislative improvement of advance living wills).
